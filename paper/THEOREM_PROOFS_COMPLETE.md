## 1. Preliminaries & Operator Definitions

### Definition 1 (Atanassov Intuitionistic Fuzzy Simplex & Evidential Projection)
Let $U$ be a universe of discourse (e.g., multimodal patient encounters). An **Atanassov Intuitionistic Fuzzy Number (IFN)** on a clinical hypothesis is a triplet:
$$\tilde{\alpha} = \langle \mu, \nu, \pi \rangle \in \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}},$$
where the admissible closed **Intuitionistic Fuzzy Simplex** $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}} \subset [0, 1]^3$ and its open interior $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ}$ are defined by:
$$\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}} = \left\{ (\mu, \nu, \pi) \in \mathbb{R}^3 \;\middle|\; \mu \ge 0,\; \nu \ge 0,\; \pi \ge 0,\; \mu + \nu + \pi = 1 \right\}, \qquad \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ} = \left\{ (\mu, \nu, \pi) \in \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}} \;\middle|\; \mu > 0,\; \nu > 0,\; \pi > 0 \right\}.$$
Here, $\mu \in [0, 1]$ denotes the **support membership degree**, $\nu \in [0, 1]$ denotes the **refutation non-membership degree**, and $\pi = 1 - \mu - \nu \in [0, 1]$ quantifies the **epistemic hesitancy (vacuity / ignorance) degree**. Following Xu and Yager (2006), the **net support score** $S(\tilde{\alpha})$, **non-vacuity (certainty) mass** $r(\tilde{\alpha})$, and **pignistic expected probability** $P(\tilde{\alpha})$ are defined as:
$$S(\tilde{\alpha}) = \mu - \nu \in [-1, 1], \qquad r(\tilde{\alpha}) = \mu + \nu = 1 - \pi \in [0, 1], \qquad P(\tilde{\alpha}) = \mu + \frac{1}{2}\pi \in [0, 1],$$
where `expected_probability()`, `IFSChoquetFusion`, and `UnifiedDeepFuzzyModel` export unclipped $P(\tilde{\alpha}) \in [0, 1]$ preserving tail rankings. Straight-through $[\varepsilon_{\mathrm{BCE}}, 1 - \varepsilon_{\mathrm{BCE}}]$ clamping ($\varepsilon_{\mathrm{BCE}} = 10^{-4}$ for `float16` and $10^{-6}$ for `bfloat16`/`float32`/`float64`) is applied inside logarithmic operations (`IntuitionisticFuzzyLoss` cross-entropy and fallback `torch.logit` when `use_tsk_head=False`). Half-precision (`float16`/`bfloat16`) and `torch.autocast` operators, models, and losses execute internal evidence, simplex subtraction, fusion, and TSK pathways in working precision (`float64`, or `float32` on MPS) with cancellation-free hesitancy preservation and fan-in-bounded backward casts.

Given modality $m \in \{1, \dots, M\}$ with sanitized feature vector $\tilde{x}_m = o_m \cdot \operatorname{nan\_to\_num}(x_m, \mathrm{nan}=0, \mathrm{posinf}=0, \mathrm{neginf}=0) \in \mathbb{R}^{D_m}$ and binary acquisition indicator $o_m \in \{0, 1\}$, let $u_m \in \mathbb{R}^{D_m^{\mathrm{enc}}}$ denote the encoder input ($u_m = \tilde{x}_m$ with $D_m^{\mathrm{enc}} = D_m$ when `IntuitionisticFuzzyEncoder` is called standalone, or $u_m = [\tilde{t}_m \parallel \hat{x}_m]$ with $D_m^{\mathrm{enc}} = d_e + D_m$ when concatenating the fuzzy-rough refined modality token $\tilde{t}_m \in \mathbb{R}^{d_e}$ and feature-gated input $\hat{x}_m \in \mathbb{R}^{D_m}$ in `UnifiedDeepFuzzyModel`, using exclusively per-sample linear/GELU or sample-wise `LayerNorm` transformations without cross-sample `BatchNorm` coupling). `IntuitionisticFuzzyEncoder` generates non-negative directional evidence via a two-layer GELU backbone $h_m(u_m)$ with linear residual skip connections $s_\mu(u_m), s_\nu(u_m)$ and softplus activations:
$$e_m^+ = o_m \cdot \operatorname{Softplus}\!\left(f_m^+(u_m) + s_\mu(u_m)\right) \ge 0, \qquad e_m^- = o_m \cdot \operatorname{Softplus}\!\left(f_m^-(u_m) + s_\nu(u_m)\right) \ge 0,$$
and projects $(e_m^+, e_m^-)$ onto $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ with learnable scalar prior vacuity mass $\pi_m^{\mathrm{prior}} = \operatorname{Softplus}(\tau_{\pi,m}) + \epsilon_0 > 0$ ($\epsilon_0 = 10^{-4}$):
$$\mu_m^{\mathrm{raw}} = \frac{e_m^+}{e_m^+ + e_m^- + \pi_m^{\mathrm{prior}}}, \qquad \nu_m^{\mathrm{raw}} = \frac{e_m^-}{e_m^+ + e_m^- + \pi_m^{\mathrm{prior}}}, \qquad \pi_m^{\mathrm{raw}} = 1 - \mu_m^{\mathrm{raw}} - \nu_m^{\mathrm{raw}} = \frac{\pi_m^{\mathrm{prior}}}{e_m^+ + e_m^- + \pi_m^{\mathrm{prior}}}. \tag{1}$$
In `UnifiedDeepFuzzyModel`, `FuzzyRoughAttentionGate` modulates certainty by $\rho_m^{\mathrm{FR}} = \operatorname{clamp}(1 - 0.12\,\operatorname{BND}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_m, 0.75, 1.0) \in [0.75, 1.0]$ ($\rho_m^{\mathrm{FR}} = 1$ in standalone encoding), yielding $\mu_m = \rho_m^{\mathrm{FR}} \mu_m^{\mathrm{raw}}$, $\nu_m = \rho_m^{\mathrm{FR}} \nu_m^{\mathrm{raw}}$, and $\pi_m = 1 - \mu_m - \nu_m \ge \pi_m^{\mathrm{raw}}$. In exact real arithmetic, because $\operatorname{Softplus}(\cdot) > 0$, $\pi_m^{\mathrm{prior}} > 0$, and $\rho_m^{\mathrm{FR}} \in [0.75, 1.0]$, any acquired modality ($o_m = 1$) maps strictly into the open simplex interior $\tilde{\alpha}_m \in \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ}$ ($\mu_m > 0, \nu_m > 0, \pi_m \in (0, 1)$, $r_m = 1 - \pi_m \in (0, 1)$), whereas any missing modality ($o_m = 0$) collapses identically to the vacuous state $\langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle$ with $r_m = 0$.

### Definition 2 (Non-Additive Monotone Choquet Intuitionistic Fuzzy Operator $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}$)
Let $\{\tilde{\alpha}_m = \langle \mu_m, \nu_m, \pi_m \rangle\}_{m=1}^M \subset \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ be $M$ modality IFNs. Let $r_m = 1 - \pi_m = \mu_m + \nu_m \in [0, 1]$ denote the non-vacuity mass of modality $m$, and let $\mathcal{O} = \{m \in \{1, \dots, M\} : r_m > 0\}$ be the set of active (non-vacuous) modalities with active indicator $a_m = \mathbb{I}(r_m > 0) = \mathbb{I}(\mu_m > 0 \lor \nu_m > 0) \in \{0, 1\}$ (for real-arithmetic outputs of Definition 1, $a_m = o_m$ so $\mathcal{O}$ coincides with the acquired set $\mathcal{A} = \{m : o_m = 1\}$; for general standalone inputs on $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$, $\mathcal{O} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$).

1. **Restricted Base Measure & Certainty-Ordered Non-Additive Monotone Choquet Capacity Increments**:
   Let $g_m^0 = \operatorname{Softplus}(\theta_m) + \epsilon_g > 0$ ($\epsilon_g = 10^{-3}$) be learnable modality utility densities. When $\mathcal{O} \neq \emptyset$, the reliability-modulated base measure restricted to $\mathcal{O}$ is:
   $$\bar{g}_m = \frac{g_m^0 r_m a_m}{\sum_{j=1}^M g_j^0 r_j a_j} \in [0, 1], \qquad \sum_{m=1}^M \bar{g}_m = 1. \tag{2}$$
   Let $\sigma$ be the stable permutation (`torch.argsort(..., descending=True, stable=True)`) sorting modalities in descending order of active certainty $s_m = r_m a_m$: $s_{\sigma(1)} \ge s_{\sigma(2)} \ge \dots \ge s_{\sigma(M)}$ (ties among equal certainties are resolved deterministically by canonical modality index order), and let $E_k = \{\sigma(1), \dots, \sigma(k)\}$ with $E_0 = \emptyset$. For learnable synergy parameter $\eta \in \mathbb{R}$ (`choquet_lambda_raw`, with $\lambda \equiv p - 1 = 0 \iff \eta = 0$) and exponent $p = \exp(\kappa \tanh(\eta)) \in (e^{-\kappa}, e^\kappa) \subset (0, \infty)$ ($\kappa = 0.35$; all algebraic results below hold for any $p > 0$), the monotone non-additive fuzzy capacity is $G_p(E_k) = \left(\sum_{j=1}^k \bar{g}_{\sigma(j)}\right)^p$, inducing certainty-ordered non-additive Choquet capacity increments:
   $$w_{\sigma(k)} = G_p(E_k) - G_p(E_{k-1}) \ge 0, \qquad \sum_{m=1}^M w_m = G_p(E_M) - G_p(\emptyset) = 1. \tag{3}$$
   If $\mathcal{O} = \emptyset$ (all modalities vacuous/missing), we set $\tilde{\alpha}_{\mathrm{fused}} = \langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle$.

2. **Observed-Pair Intuitionistic Fuzzy Conflict & Exponential Discounting**:
   For any pair $(m, n)$ with $m \neq n$, the cross-polarity contradiction mass is $C_{m,n} = \mu_m \nu_n + \nu_m \mu_n = \frac{1}{2}\big(r_m r_n - S(\tilde{\alpha}_m)S(\tilde{\alpha}_n)\big) \in [0, 1]$. The observed-pair conflict $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}$ and discount factor $d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}})$ with penalty $\gamma \ge 0$ are:
   $$\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = \frac{\sum_{m=1}^M \sum_{n \neq m} a_m a_n (\mu_m \nu_n + \nu_m \mu_n)}{\max\left(1,\; \sum_{m=1}^M \sum_{n \neq m} a_m a_n\right)} \in [0, 1], \qquad d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}) = \exp(-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}) \in (0, 1]. \tag{4}$$
   Each modality IFN is discounted by $d = d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}})$:
   $$\hat{\mu}_m = d \mu_m, \qquad \hat{\nu}_m = d \nu_m, \qquad \hat{\pi}_m = 1 - \hat{\mu}_m - \hat{\nu}_m = 1 - d r_m. \tag{5}$$

3. **Capacity-Weighted Geometric-Hesitancy Intuitionistic Fuzzy Aggregation (Hesitancy-Preserving IFWA)**:
   Inspired by Xu's (2007) intuitionistic fuzzy multiplicative aggregation, the fused IFN $\tilde{\alpha}_{\mathrm{fused}} = \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}(\tilde{\alpha}_1, \dots, \tilde{\alpha}_M) = \langle \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} \rangle$ applies capacity-weighted geometric pooling to $(1 - \hat{\mu}_m)$ and $\hat{\pi}_m$:
   $$\mu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1 - \prod_{m=1}^M (1 - \hat{\mu}_m)^{w_m}, \qquad \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = \prod_{m=1}^M (\hat{\pi}_m)^{w_m}, \qquad \nu_{\mathrm{fused}} = \prod_{m=1}^M (1 - \hat{\mu}_m)^{w_m} - \prod_{m=1}^M (\hat{\pi}_m)^{w_m} = 1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}} - \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}. \tag{6}$$
   *Advantage over Standard IFWA*: Standard IFWA (Xu, 2007) defines $\nu_{\mathrm{IFWA}} = \prod_{m=1}^M (\hat{\nu}_m)^{w_m}$, which suffers from a **zero-veto refutation collapse**: if any single positive-weight modality has $\hat{\nu}_m = 0$ (e.g., $\langle 0.8, 0.0, 0.2\rangle$), then $\nu_{\mathrm{IFWA}} = 0$ regardless of how strongly other modalities refute the hypothesis. By contrast, whenever $1 - \hat{\mu}_j > 0$ ($\hat{\mu}_j < 1$) for all positive-weight active modalities $j \in \mathcal{O}$ (which holds unconditionally for all real-arithmetic outputs of Definition 1 since $\pi_j \ge \pi_j^{\mathrm{raw}} > 0 \implies \hat{\mu}_j \le \mu_j < 1$) and at least one positive-weight active modality has $\hat{\nu}_{m^*} > 0$, pooling $(1 - \hat{\mu}_m)$ and $\hat{\pi}_m$ in Eq. (6) guarantees:
   $$\nu_{\mathrm{fused}} = \left[\prod_{j \in \mathcal{O}} (1 - \hat{\mu}_j)^{w_j}\right]\left[1 - \prod_{j \in \mathcal{O}} \left(1 - \frac{\hat{\nu}_j}{1 - \hat{\mu}_j}\right)^{w_j}\right] > 0,$$
   preserving opposing evidence across modalities while making $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = \prod_m (1 - d r_m)^{w_m}$ depend exclusively on non-vacuity masses $\{r_m\}$ and conflict discount $d$.

## 2. Rigorous Proofs of the Core Mathematical Theorems

### Theorem 1 (Intuitionistic Fuzzy Simplex Closure, Open-Interior Smoothness a.e., & Tie/Active-Set Geometry)
*For any collection of input modality representations $\{\tilde{\alpha}_m = \langle \mu_m, \nu_m, \pi_m \rangle\}_{m=1}^M \subset \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ (and any arbitrary finite or non-finite inputs sanitized radially onto $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ inside `IFSChoquetFusion`) and any parameters $(\boldsymbol{\theta}, \eta)$, exponent $p > 0$, and conflict penalty $\gamma \ge 0$, in exact real arithmetic the fused output $\tilde{\alpha}_{\mathrm{fused}} = \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}(\tilde{\alpha}_1, \dots, \tilde{\alpha}_M)$ strictly resides within the closed Atanassov Intuitionistic Fuzzy Simplex $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ ($\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} \in [0, 1]$ and $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}} + \nu_{\mathrm{fused}} + \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1$). Moreover:*
1. **(Strict Interior Hesitancy on Encoder Outputs)**: *Whenever all active modalities $m \in \mathcal{O}$ satisfy $\pi_m \in (0, 1)$ ($r_m \in (0, 1)$, as guaranteed unconditionally in real arithmetic by Eq. (1)), the fused output satisfies strict interior hesitancy:*
   $$\mu_{\mathrm{fused}} \in [0, 1), \qquad \nu_{\mathrm{fused}} \in [0, 1), \qquad \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} \in (0, 1], \qquad \mu_{\mathrm{fused}} + \nu_{\mathrm{fused}} + \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1.$$
2. **(Piecewise $C^\infty$ Smoothness on Fixed Active Set $\mathcal{O}$ & Tie/Active-Set Transition Behavior)**: *For any **fixed** non-empty active modality set $\mathcal{O} = \{m : r_m > 0\}$ ($|\mathcal{O}| \ge 1$), let $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{tie}} = \bigcup_{\substack{m, n \in \mathcal{O} \\ m < n}} \{r_m = r_n > 0\}$ denote the Lebesgue measure-zero tie hyperplane union of equal positive non-vacuity masses. On the open active-modality interior $(\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ})^{|\mathcal{O}|}$, $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}$ is globally $C^\infty$ smooth when $\eta = 0$ ($p = 1$, additive capacity $\lambda \equiv p - 1 = 0$), and when $\eta \neq 0$ ($p \neq 1$, $\lambda \neq 0$) it is **$C^\infty$ smoothly differentiable on every open strict-ordering chamber of $(\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ})^{|\mathcal{O}|} \setminus \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{tie}}$** (hence smoothly differentiable almost everywhere on $(\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ})^{|\mathcal{O}|}$ at fixed $\mathcal{O}$). Across any tied positive-certainty block $B(r) = \{j \in \mathcal{O} : r_j = r > 0\}$ ($|B(r)| \ge 2$) when $p \neq 1$, $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$ is globally continuous ($C^0$, piecewise $C^\infty$ with continuous value and one-sided directional kinks); $(\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \nu_{\mathrm{fused}})$ are continuous across $B(r)$ **if and only if** either all $j \in B(r)$ share equal support $\mu_j$ or some active modality $m^* \in \mathcal{O}$ attains the absorbing unit positive-evidence state $\hat{\mu}_{m^*} := d\mu_{m^*} = 1$ (equivalently $\mu_{m^*} = 1$ with $\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = 0$, forcing $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}} \to 1$ and $\nu_{\mathrm{fused}} \to 0$ continuously along every approaching sequence). On the non-absorbing domain $\{\hat{\mu}_m < 1 \;\forall m \in \mathcal{O}\}$ (which covers all $\gamma > 0$ with $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$ and all of $(\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^\circ)^{|\mathcal{O}|}$), unequal $\mu_m \neq \mu_n$ inside $B(r)$ always produce a finite Choquet capacity-swap jump resolved deterministically by canonical modality index order via stable `argsort`. Finally, across an **active-set transition** $r_u : 0 \to \epsilon > 0$ adding an active modality, when $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = 0$ (including $|\mathcal{O}| = 1 \to 2$, where $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O} \cup \{u\}} = O(\epsilon) \to 0$) the transition is continuous in $d$ and, provided the pre-existing certainty ordering or equal-$\mu_j$ tie-block structure is preserved, in fused outputs; when $|\mathcal{O}| \ge 2$ with existing cross-polarity conflict $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$, the denominator $|\mathcal{O}|(|\mathcal{O}|-1)$ in Eq. (4) causes a $\gamma$-independent jump in $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}$ scaling by $(|\mathcal{O}|-1)/(|\mathcal{O}|+1)$ and a uniform $|\Delta d|=O(\gamma / |\mathcal{O}|)$ jump in $d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}})$ across $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ for $\gamma\ge0$, independent of the certainty ordering. On any bounded-certainty region $\{r_m \le 1 - \delta\}^{|\mathcal{O}|}$ ($\delta \in (0,1)$), power bases remain bounded below by $\delta > 0$. Each fixed bounded-certainty region gives an $O_\delta(\gamma / |\mathcal{O}|)$ jump in $(\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \pi_{\mathrm{fused}})$ provided the pre-existing certainty ordering, or equal-$\mu_j$ tie-block structure, among active modalities in $\mathcal{O}$ is preserved. A simultaneous reversal of a pre-existing unequal-$\mu$ tie at $p\ne1$ adds the finite capacity-swap jump even at $\gamma=0$. The union $\bigcup_{\delta\in(0,1)}\{r_m\le1-\delta\}^{|\mathcal{O}|}$ covers $(\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ})^{|\mathcal{O}|}$. True compact interior exhaustion instead uses $\{\mu_m,\nu_m,\pi_m\ge1/k\}^{|\mathcal{O}|}$ as $k\to\infty$; every acquired Definition 1 sample lies in such a set for some sample-dependent $\delta_i>0$. Conversely, on closed crisp-certainty boundary faces $r_j = 1$ ($\pi_j = 0$) and $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$, let $W_\pi = \sum_{j \in \mathcal{O}: r_j = 1} w_j \in (0, 1]$ and $W_\mu = \sum_{j \in \mathcal{O}: \mu_j = 1} w_j \in [0, 1)$ (strictly $< 1$ because $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$ requires some $n \in \mathcal{O}$ with $\nu_n > 0$ and $\mu_n < 1$). As $\gamma \to 0^+$, vanishing bases $1 - d = \Theta(\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}})$ yield $|\Delta \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_\pi})$ (sublinear Hölder for $W_\pi < 1$, linear $\Theta(\gamma)$ for $W_\pi = 1$), $|\Delta \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_\mu})$ if $W_\mu > 0$ and $O_{\delta_\mu}(\gamma / |\mathcal{O}|)$ if $W_\mu = 0$ ($\delta_\mu = 1 - \max_{m \in \mathcal{O}} \mu_m > 0$), $|\Delta \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_*})$ whenever $W_* < 1$ (with guaranteed non-cancellation $A_\mu > A_\pi > 0$ at $W_\mu = W_\pi \in (0, 1)$, and $O_{\delta_\mu}(\gamma)$ at $W_\mu = 0, W_\pi = 1$), and $\|\Delta \tilde{\alpha}_{\mathrm{fused}}\|_\infty = \Theta(\gamma^{W_*})$ with $W_* = W_\mu$ if $W_\mu > 0$ else $W_\pi \in (0, 1]$; setting $\gamma = 0$ removes these active-set discount jumps.*

#### Proof of Theorem 1
1. **Case 1 ($\mathcal{O} = \emptyset$, All Modalities Missing)**:
   By definition, $\tilde{\alpha}_{\mathrm{fused}} = \langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle \in \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$.
2. **Case 2 ($\mathcal{O} \neq \emptyset$, Simplex Closure & Interior Bounds)**:
   - Because $\bar{g}_{\sigma(j)} \ge 0$ and $\sum_{j=1}^M \bar{g}_{\sigma(j)} = 1$, the cumulative sums $S_k = \sum_{j=1}^k \bar{g}_{\sigma(j)}$ satisfy $0 = S_0 \le S_1 \le \dots \le S_M = 1$. Since $t \mapsto t^p$ is strictly increasing on $[0, 1]$ for all $p > 0$, we have $w_{\sigma(k)} = S_k^p - S_{k-1}^p \ge 0$ and $\sum_{k=1}^M w_{\sigma(k)} = S_M^p - S_0^p = 1^p - 0^p = 1$.
   - For any closed-simplex input $(\mu_m, \nu_m, \pi_m) \in \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ ($r_m = \mu_m + \nu_m \in [0, 1]$) and conflict discount $d = \exp(-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}) \in (0, 1]$, the discounted components in Eq. (5) satisfy:
     $$0 \le \hat{\mu}_m = d \mu_m \le d(\mu_m + \nu_m) = d r_m \le 1 \implies 0 \le \hat{\pi}_m = 1 - d r_m \le 1 - \hat{\mu}_m \le 1.$$
   - Because $w_m \ge 0$ and $\sum_{m=1}^M w_m = 1$ (with $w_u = 0$, $1 - \hat{\mu}_u = 1$, and $\hat{\pi}_u = 1$ yielding $1^0 = 1$ for vacuous modalities $u \notin \mathcal{O}$), monotonicity of the weighted geometric mean on $[0, 1]$ preserves order across the entire closed simplex $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$:
     $$0 \le \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = \prod_{m=1}^M (\hat{\pi}_m)^{w_m} \le \prod_{m=1}^M (1 - \hat{\mu}_m)^{w_m} = 1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}} \le 1.$$
   - Therefore, $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1 - \prod_{m=1}^M (1 - \hat{\mu}_m)^{w_m} \in [0, 1]$, $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}} \in [0, 1]$, and $\nu_{\mathrm{fused}} = (1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}) - \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} \in [0, 1]$, with $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}} + \nu_{\mathrm{fused}} + \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1$ holding identically across all of $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$. Whenever $r_m \in (0, 1)$ for all $m \in \mathcal{O}$, $\hat{\pi}_m = 1 - d r_m > 0$ and $1 - \hat{\mu}_m \ge \hat{\pi}_m > 0$, ensuring $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}} \in (0, 1]$ and $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \nu_{\mathrm{fused}} \in [0, 1)$.
3. **Open-Interior Differentiability, Multiway Tie-Block Continuity, & Active-Set Boundary**:
   - On $(\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ})^{|\mathcal{O}|}$ with **fixed** active set $\mathcal{O}$, $r_m \in (0, 1)$ is strictly positive for all $m \in \mathcal{O}$, so $a_m \equiv 1$ is locally constant, $\sum_{j \in \mathcal{O}} g_j^0 r_j > 0$, and the bases $1 - \hat{\mu}_m \ge \hat{\pi}_m = 1 - d r_m \in (0, 1)$ lie strictly inside $(0, 1)$ where $(x, w) \mapsto x^w = \exp(w \ln x)$ is real-analytic ($C^\infty$).
   - When $p = 1$ ($\eta = 0$), $w_{\sigma(k)} = S_k - S_{k-1} = \bar{g}_{\sigma(k)}$ cancels the sorting permutation $\sigma$ identically ($w_m = \bar{g}_m$), so $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}$ is globally $C^\infty$ on $(\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ})^{|\mathcal{O}|}$.
   - When $p \neq 1$ ($\eta \neq 0$), the tie locus $\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{tie}} = \bigcup_{m < n} \{r_m - r_n = 0\}$ is a finite union of codimension-1 affine hyperplanes of Lebesgue measure zero. Moreover, every active modality $k \in \mathcal{O}$ has $\bar{g}_k > 0$ and strictly positive weight $w_k > 0$ (because $t \mapsto t^p$ is strictly increasing on $[0, 1]$ for all $p > 0$), and $d = \exp(-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}) \in [e^{-\gamma}, 1] > 0$ for all finite $\gamma \ge 0$ in exact real arithmetic. In IEEE-754 `float64`, $d>0$ whenever $\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}\le709$. For sufficiently extreme standalone inputs with $\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}>745$, $d$ can cleanly underflow to zero, yielding $\hat{\pi}_m=1$ without `NaN`. On every connected open strict chamber $\{r_{\sigma(1)} > \dots > r_{\sigma(|\mathcal{O}|)} > 0\}$ of $(\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}^{\circ})^{|\mathcal{O}|} \setminus \mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{tie}}$, $\sigma$ is locally constant and $S_k = \sum_{j=1}^k \bar{g}_{\sigma(j)} \in (0, 1]$, so $S_k^p$, $w_m$, $d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}})$, and $\tilde{\alpha}_{\mathrm{fused}}$ are $C^\infty$ smooth. Consider any tied positive-certainty block $B(r) = \{j \in \mathcal{O} : r_j = r > 0\}$ ($|B(r)| \ge 2$) occupying contiguous sorted ranks $\{k_1, \dots, k_2\}$ with $S_{k_1 - 1}$ and $S_{k_2} = S_{k_1 - 1} + \sum_{j \in B(r)} \bar{g}_j$ invariant under arbitrary permutations of $B(r)$. Thus $\sum_{j \in B(r)} w_j = S_{k_2}^p - S_{k_1 - 1}^p$ is permutation-invariant while $(1 - d r_j) = 1 - d r$ is identical across $B(r)$, giving $\prod_{j \in B(r)} (1 - d r_j)^{w_j} = (1 - d r)^{\sum_{j \in B(r)} w_j}$ and global $C^0$ continuity of $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$ across any multiway tie $B(r)$. For $(\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \nu_{\mathrm{fused}})$ across $B(r)$, let $m, n \in B(r)$ occupy adjacent ranks within $B(r)$ with cumulative predecessor density $a \ge 0$ and normalized densities $b_m = \bar{g}_m > 0$, $b_n = \bar{g}_n > 0$; transposing $(m, n)$ shifts $(w_m, w_n)$ to $(w_m + \delta, w_n - \delta)$ where $\delta := (a + b_m + b_n)^p + a^p - (a + b_m)^p - (a + b_n)^p = p(p-1)\int_0^{b_m}\int_0^{b_n} (a+s+t)^{p-2}\,dt\,ds \neq 0$ unconditionally for all $p \neq 1$, while leaving all other $w_k$ ($k \notin \{m, n\}$) and $d$ invariant. If some $m^* \in \mathcal{O}$ attains the absorbing vertex $\hat{\mu}_{m^*} = d\mu_{m^*} = 1$ (iff $\mu_{m^*}=1$ and $\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = 0$), then along any sequence $\{\tilde{\alpha}^{(t)}\} \subset (\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}} \setminus \{\langle 0,0,1\rangle\})^{|\mathcal{O}|}$ converging to the tie configuration, $\bar{g}_{m^*}^{(t)} \to \bar{g}_{m^*} > 0$ implies $\bar{g}_{m^*}^{(t)} \ge c_0 := \frac{1}{2}\bar{g}_{m^*} > 0$ eventually; strict monotonicity of $s \mapsto s^p$ on $[0,1]$ then gives a uniform lower bound $w_{m^*}^{(t)} \ge \min_{a \in [0, 1-c_0]}((a+c_0)^p - a^p) =: w_{\min} > 0$ across all sorting permutations $\sigma^{(t)}$ (even when $m^* \in B(r)$), so $\hat{\mu}_{m^*}^{(t)} \to 1^-$ forces $0 \le 1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}^{(t)} \le (1 - \hat{\mu}_{m^*}^{(t)})^{w_{m^*}^{(t)}} \le (1 - \hat{\mu}_{m^*}^{(t)})^{w_{\min}} \to 0$, proving $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}^{(t)} \to 1 = \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}$ and $\nu_{\mathrm{fused}}^{(t)} \to 0 = \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}$ continuously across $B(r)$; conversely, on the non-absorbing domain $\{\hat{\mu}_k < 1 \;\forall k \in \mathcal{O}\}$, swapping adjacent $m, n \in B(r)$ multiplies $1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}} > 0$ by $\big(\frac{1 - d\mu_m}{1 - d\mu_n}\big)^\delta$, which equals $1$ iff $\mu_m = \mu_n$ (so $(\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \nu_{\mathrm{fused}})$ is continuous across $B(r)$ iff either all $j \in B(r)$ share equal $\mu_j$ or some $m^* \in \mathcal{O}$ has $d\mu_{m^*} = 1$). Finally, if an inactive modality $u$ transitions from $r_u = 0$ to $r_u = \epsilon > 0$, $w_u = O(\epsilon) \to 0$ keeps Choquet capacities continuous; when $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = 0$ (including $|\mathcal{O}| = 1 \to 2$, where pairwise conflict is at most $\epsilon \to 0$), $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O} \cup \{u\}} \to 0$ and $d$ varies continuously; fused outputs do too if the pre-existing certainty ordering or equal-$\mu_j$ tie-block structure is preserved. However, when $|\mathcal{O}| \ge 2$ with existing active conflict $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$, $|\mathcal{O}| \to |\mathcal{O}| + 1$ causes a $\gamma$-independent denominator jump in Eq. (4) that scales $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}$ by $(|\mathcal{O}| - 1) / (|\mathcal{O}| + 1)$ to limit $\mathcal{C}_+ = \frac{|\mathcal{O}|-1}{|\mathcal{O}|+1}\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} < \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} =: \mathcal{C}_-$, yielding a uniform jump $|\Delta d| = |e^{-\gamma\mathcal{C}_+} - e^{-\gamma\mathcal{C}_-}| = O(\gamma / |\mathcal{O}|)$ in $d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}})$ (with the discount-induced contribution vanishing from fused outputs at $\gamma = 0$). When all active certainties stay in a bounded-certainty region $r_m \le 1 - \delta_{\mathrm{int}}$ ($\delta_{\mathrm{int}} \in (0,1)$) and the pre-existing certainty ordering or equal-$\mu_j$ tie-block structure in $\mathcal{O}$ is preserved, $1 - d\mu_m \ge 1 - dr_m \ge \delta_{\mathrm{int}} > 0$ bounds the Lipschitz constant of $t \mapsto t^{w_m}$ on $[\delta_{\mathrm{int}}, 1]$, so fused outputs inherit an $O_{\delta_{\mathrm{int}}}(\gamma / |\mathcal{O}|)$ jump. A simultaneous reversal of a pre-existing unequal-$\mu$ tie at $p\ne1$ adds a finite capacity-swap jump even at $\gamma=0$. Conversely, under the same ordering proviso, at closed crisp-certainty boundary faces $r_j = 1$ ($\pi_j = 0$) and $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$, let $S_\pi = \{j \in \mathcal{O}: r_j = 1\}$ and $S_\mu = \{j \in \mathcal{O}: \mu_j = 1\} \subseteq S_\pi$ with $W_\pi = \sum_{j \in S_\pi} w_j \in (0, 1]$ and $W_\mu = \sum_{j \in S_\mu} w_j \in [0, 1)$ (strictly $< 1$ because opposing $\nu_n > 0 \implies \mu_n < 1$ forces $w_n > 0$). As $\gamma \to 0^+$, $(1 - d_\pm) = \gamma \mathcal{C}_\pm + O(\gamma^2)$ gives $|\Delta \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_\pi})$ and $|\Delta \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_\mu})$ if $W_\mu > 0$ else $O_{\delta_\mu}(\gamma / |\mathcal{O}|)$ ($\delta_\mu = 1 - \max_{m \in \mathcal{O}} \mu_m > 0$). For $\nu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}} - \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$, $|\Delta \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_\mu})$ if $0 < W_\mu < W_\pi$, $|\Delta \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_\pi})$ if $0 = W_\mu < W_\pi < 1$, and $O_{\delta_\mu}(\gamma)$ if $W_\mu = 0, W_\pi = 1$. In the remaining tied-exponent case $0 < W_\mu = W_\pi =: W_* < 1$, $S_\mu \subseteq S_\pi$ with $w_j > 0 \;\forall j \in \mathcal{O}$ forces $S_\mu = S_\pi =: S$ ($\nu_j = 0 \;\forall j \in S$ and $r_k < 1 \;\forall k \in \mathcal{O} \setminus S$). Since $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$, some $n \in \mathcal{O} \setminus S$ satisfies $\nu_n > 0$, so $1 - \mu_k = (1 - r_k) + \nu_k \ge 1 - r_k > 0$ for all $k \in \mathcal{O} \setminus S$ with strict inequality $1 - \mu_n > 1 - r_n$ ($w_n > 0$), giving $A_\mu := \prod_{k \in \mathcal{O}\setminus S}(1 - \mu_k)^{w_k} > A_\pi := \prod_{k \in \mathcal{O}\setminus S}(1 - r_k)^{w_k} > 0$. Because $w_u^+ \to 0$ as $r_u \to 0^+$, the leading term of $\Delta \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}$ as $\gamma \to 0^+$ is $-(A_\mu - A_\pi)(\mathcal{C}_-^{W_*} - \mathcal{C}_+^{W_*})\gamma^{W_*} \neq 0$, ruling out cancellation and proving $|\Delta \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_*})$ and $\|\Delta \tilde{\alpha}_{\mathrm{fused}}\|_\infty = \Theta(\gamma^{W_*})$ across all $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$ boundary configurations. Note that Eq. (1) guarantees $o_m = 1 \implies \mathcal{O} = \mathcal{A}$ stays fixed during inference unless acquisition indicators change. $\blacksquare$

#### Remark 1 (Closed-Vertex Polarity Asymmetry, Input Sanitization & Working-Precision `float64` / MPS `float32` Realization)
1. **Closed-Vertex Boundary Behavior ($\langle 1, 0, 0\rangle$ Positive Absorbing Limit vs. $\langle 0, 1, 0\rangle$ Residual Formulation) & Malformed-Input Projection**:
   For real-arithmetic encoder outputs of Definition 1, $\pi_m \ge \pi_m^{\mathrm{raw}} > 0$ guarantees $r_m < 1$, so closed crisp-certainty boundary faces on $\partial \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ with $r_m=1$ ($\pi_m = 0$) are never encountered during end-to-end neural forward passes. For standalone callers passing malformed inputs directly into `IFSChoquetFusion` (`NaN`, $\pm\infty$, negative entries, $\mu_m + \nu_m > 1$, or mismatched $\pi_m$), `_sanitize_input_ifn_f64` radially projects $(\mu_m, \nu_m)$ onto $\{\mu, \nu \ge 0,\; \mu + \nu \le 1\}$ and recomputes $\pi_m = 1 - \max(\mu_m, \nu_m) - \min(\mu_m, \nu_m)$ across both single-observed ($|\mathcal{O}|=1$) and multi-modality ($|\mathcal{O}|\ge 2$) branches while acting as the identity on valid closed-simplex inputs. When valid closed-simplex boundary triplets in $\partial \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ are passed with $|\mathcal{O}| \ge 2$ at $d = 1$ ($\gamma = 0$ or $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = 0$), Eq. (6) exhibits an intentional design asymmetry between the two crisp vertices:
   - *Positive Crisp Vertex $\langle 1, 0, 0\rangle$ (Absorbing Limit)*: If an active modality $m^* \in \mathcal{O}$ with $w_{m^*} > 0$ has $\hat{\mu}_{m^*} = 1$ ($\hat{\nu}_{m^*} = \hat{\pi}_{m^*} = 0$), multiplicative pooling of $(1 - \hat{\mu}_m)^{w_m}$ gives $(1 - \hat{\mu}_{m^*})^{w_{m^*}} = 0 \implies \mu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1$ and $\nu_{\mathrm{fused}} = \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = 0$ (`any_mu_one` branch in `IFSChoquetFusion` inside `deepfuzzy/operators.py`), matching the positive absorbing property of Xu's (2007) IFWA. Importantly, whenever $\gamma > 0$ and any other active modality carries opposing refutation $\nu_n > 0$, cross-modal conflict $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$ yields $d = e^{-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}} < 1 \implies \hat{\mu}_{m^*} = d < 1$ and $\hat{\pi}_{m^*} = 1 - d > 0$, lifting even $\langle 1, 0, 0\rangle + \langle 0, 1, 0\rangle$ out of the boundary vertex to interior hesitancy $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1 - e^{-\gamma} > 0$.
   - *Negative Crisp Vertex $\langle 0, 1, 0\rangle$ (Hesitancy-Preserving Residual)*: Because Eq. (6) replaces standard IFWA's $\prod_m \hat{\nu}_m^{w_m}$ with the residual $\nu_{\mathrm{fused}} = (1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}) - \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$ to eliminate zero-veto refutation collapse when another modality has $\hat{\nu}_j = 0$, an input $\tilde{\alpha}_{m^*} = \langle 0, 1, 0\rangle$ ($w_{m^*} > 0, d = 1$, with $\hat{\mu}_j < 1 \;\forall j$) drives $\hat{\pi}_{m^*} = 0 \implies \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = 0$ (`any_pi_zero` branch), while $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1 - \prod_{j \in \mathcal{O} \setminus \{m^*\}}(1 - \hat{\mu}_j)^{w_j}$ and $\nu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}$ retain finite positive support from any modality with $\hat{\mu}_j > 0$ (e.g., fusing $\langle 0.1, 0, 0.9\rangle$ and $\langle 0, 1, 0\rangle$ at $\gamma = 0$ yields $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = 0$, $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1 - 0.9^{w_1} > 0$, and $\nu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 0.9^{w_1} < 1$, reaching $\langle 0, 1, 0\rangle$ iff $\hat{\mu}_j = 0$ for all positive-weight modalities).
2. **Tie Continuity vs. Finite Capacity-Swap Jump ($p \neq 1$) & Working-Precision `pre-log cap` Stability**:
   - *Tie Continuity vs. Capacity-Swap Behavior*: Whenever $p = 1$ (linear IFWA), Choquet weights reduce continuously to normalized densities across all certainty ties. For synergy exponents $p \neq 1$ and any fixed non-empty active set $\mathcal{O}$, hesitancy $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$ remains globally continuous ($C^0$) on $(\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}} \setminus \{\langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle\})^{|\mathcal{O}|}$, and $(\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}, \nu_{\mathrm{fused}})$ are continuous across a multiway tie block $B(r)$ ($|B(r)| \ge 2$) **iff** either all $j \in B(r)$ share equal $\mu_j$ or some active modality $m^* \in \mathcal{O}$ hits the absorbing state $\hat{\mu}_{m^*} = d\mu_{m^*} = 1$ ($\mu_{m^*} = 1$ and $\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = 0$). On $\{\hat{\mu}_m < 1 \;\forall m \in \mathcal{O}\}$, unequal-$\mu$ ties ($m, n \in B(r)$ with $\mu_m \neq \mu_n$) always induce a finite capacity-swap jump in $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}$ and $\nu_{\mathrm{fused}}$ ($\delta \neq 0$ for $p \neq 1$), which `IFSChoquetFusion` resolves deterministically via stable descending sorting (`stable=True`) preserving canonical modality index order. Across an active-set entry $r_u : 0 \to 0^+$, when $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = 0$ (including $|\mathcal{O}| = 1 \to 2$) $d$ and, provided the pre-existing certainty ordering or equal-$\mu_j$ tie-block structure is preserved, fused outputs vary continuously; when $|\mathcal{O}| \ge 2$ with existing conflict $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$, $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}$ undergoes a $\gamma$-independent denominator jump scaling by $(|\mathcal{O}| - 1)/(|\mathcal{O}| + 1)$ and $d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}})$ undergoes a uniform $|\Delta d|=O(\gamma / |\mathcal{O}|)$ jump for $\gamma\ge0$, independent of the certainty ordering. This induces an interior $O_\delta(\gamma / |\mathcal{O}|)$ jump in fused outputs on each $\{r_m \le 1 - \delta\}^{|\mathcal{O}|}$ ($\delta > 0$), provided the pre-existing certainty ordering or equal-$\mu_j$ tie-block structure among active modalities is preserved; simultaneous reversal of an unequal-$\mu$ tie at $p\ne1$ adds a finite capacity-swap jump even at $\gamma=0$. Under the same ordering proviso, at closed vertices with $r_j = 1$ as $\gamma \to 0^+$ it gives $|\Delta \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_\pi})$ ($W_\pi \in (0, 1]$), $|\Delta \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_\mu})$ if $W_\mu > 0$ else $O_{\delta_\mu}(\gamma / |\mathcal{O}|)$ ($W_\mu \in [0, 1)$), $|\Delta \nu_{\mathrm{fused}}| = \Theta(\gamma^{W_*})$ whenever $W_* < 1$ (with guaranteed non-cancellation $A_\mu > A_\pi > 0$ at $W_\mu = W_\pi \in (0, 1)$), and $\|\Delta \tilde{\alpha}_{\mathrm{fused}}\|_\infty = \Theta(\gamma^{W_*})$ ($W_* = W_\mu$ if $W_\mu > 0$ else $W_\pi$; sublinear Hölder whenever $W_* < 1$), while the discount-induced jump vanishes from fused outputs at $\gamma = 0$.
   - *Finite-Precision `pre-log cap` (`1 - 2^{-53}` in `float64`; `1 - 2^{-24}` on MPS `float32`) & Log-Space Stability*: In finite-precision execution, `IFSChoquetFusion` dynamically rescales $r_m$ via `frexp`/`ldexp` power-of-two shifts so $\max_m r_m \in [0.5, 1.0)$ across all positive working-precision scales (down to $5 \times 10^{-324}$ in `float64`, or positive `float32` scales on MPS). It passes valid single-observed-modality inputs ($|\mathcal{O}| = 1$) through identically with simplex-tangent backward gradients (after sanitizing any invalid simplex triplets). It applies an explicit working-precision pre-log cap of `1 - 2^{-53}` (IEEE-754 `float64`) or `1 - 2^{-24}` (MPS `float32`) to $\hat{\mu}_m$ and $q_m = \hat{\nu}_m / (1 - \hat{\mu}_m)$ before `log1p(-x)` on interior inputs ($1 - \hat{\mu}_m > 0$ and $\hat{\pi}_m > 0$) to preclude `log1p(-1.0) = -inf` while exact boundary inputs use the dedicated `any_mu_one`/`any_pi_zero` branches. It accumulates interior geometric products in canonical descending-$r_{\sigma(k)}$ order using `log1p`/`expm1` and the cancellation-free factorization $\hat{\pi}_m = (1 - \hat{\mu}_m)(1 - q_m)$.

### Theorem 2 (Exact Missing-Modality Vacuity Degeneration & Zero-Padding Invariance)
*Let $\mathcal{M} = \{1, \dots, M\}$ be the full set of modalities, let $\mathcal{U} \subset \mathcal{M}$ ($1 \le |\mathcal{U}| < M$) be the subset of unacquired (missing) modalities ($o_u = 0 \;\forall u \in \mathcal{U}$, so $\tilde{\alpha}_u = \langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle$ regardless of raw padding values $x_u \in (\mathbb{R} \cup \{\mathrm{NaN}, \pm\infty\})^{D_u}$), let $\mathcal{A} = \mathcal{M} \setminus \mathcal{U}$ ($1 \le |\mathcal{A}| < M$) be the complementary subset of acquired modalities, and let $\mathcal{O} = \{m \in \mathcal{A} : r_m > 0\} \subseteq \mathcal{A}$ be the active non-vacuous subset (for real-arithmetic outputs of Definition 1, $\mathcal{O} = \mathcal{A}$). Then for **any** conflict penalty $\gamma \ge 0$, any synergy exponent $p > 0$, and any insertion positions of the missing modalities $\mathcal{U}$ that preserve the relative index order of the acquired modalities $\mathcal{A}$ (or for arbitrary permutations when active certainties $\{r_m\}_{m \in \mathcal{O}}$ are distinct or $p = 1$), the $M$-modality operator $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}^{(M)}$ evaluated on $\{\tilde{\alpha}_m\}_{m \in \mathcal{A}} \cup \{\langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle\}_{u \in \mathcal{U}}$ is **algebraically identical** to the $|\mathcal{A}|$-modality operator $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}^{(|\mathcal{A}|)}$ restricted to $\mathcal{A}$ with density parameters $\boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathcal{A}} = (\theta_m)_{m \in \mathcal{A}}$ (and to $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}^{(|\mathcal{O}|)}$ when $\mathcal{O} \neq \emptyset$):*
$$\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}^{(M)}\left(\{\tilde{\alpha}_m\}_{m \in \mathcal{A}} \cup \{\langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle\}_{u \in \mathcal{U}};\, \boldsymbol{\theta}, \eta, \gamma\right) \equiv \mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}^{(|\mathcal{A}|)}\left(\{\tilde{\alpha}_m\}_{m \in \mathcal{A}};\, \boldsymbol{\theta}_{\mathcal{A}}, \eta, \gamma\right),$$
*and every missing modality $u \in \mathcal{U}$ (as well as any vacuous modality $m \in \mathcal{A} \setminus \mathcal{O}$) receives an exact zero Choquet weight $w_u \equiv 0$.*

#### Proof of Theorem 2
1. **Exact Vacuity Collapse & Empty-Active Case ($\mathcal{O} = \emptyset$)**:
   For every missing modality $u \in \mathcal{U}$, input sanitization $\tilde{x}_u = o_u \cdot \operatorname{nan\_to\_num}(x_u, 0, 0, 0) = \mathbf{0}_{D_u}$ and $o_u = 0$ in Eq. (1) yield $e_u^+ = e_u^- = 0$ independently of $x_u$, so $\tilde{\alpha}_u = \langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle$, $r_u = 1 - \pi_u = 0$, and $a_u = \mathbb{I}(r_u > 0) = 0$. Hence $\mathcal{O}^{(M)} = \{m \in \mathcal{M} : r_m > 0\} = \{m \in \mathcal{A} : r_m > 0\} = \mathcal{O}^{(|\mathcal{A}|)} = \mathcal{O}$. If $\mathcal{O} = \emptyset$, both $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}^{(M)}$ and $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}^{(|\mathcal{A}|)}$ return $\langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle$ with $w_m = 0$ and $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = 0$.
2. **Invariance of Restricted Base Measure & Choquet Capacity Increments ($\mathcal{O} \neq \emptyset$)**:
   Suppose $\mathcal{O} \neq \emptyset$. In Eq. (2), since $r_u a_u = 0$ for all $u \notin \mathcal{O}$ (including all $u \in \mathcal{U}$), we have $\bar{g}_u = 0$ for all $u \notin \mathcal{O}$, and for every $m \in \mathcal{O}$:
   $$\bar{g}_m^{(M)} = \frac{g_m^0 r_m}{\sum_{j \in \mathcal{O}} g_j^0 r_j + \sum_{u \notin \mathcal{O}} 0} = \bar{g}_m^{(|\mathcal{A}|)} = \bar{g}_m^{(|\mathcal{O}|)}.$$
   Under the stable descending certainty ordering $s_m = r_m a_m$, all active modalities $m \in \mathcal{O}$ ($s_m > 0$) strictly precede all inactive/missing modalities $u \notin \mathcal{O}$ ($s_u = 0$) in identical relative order. For every inactive/missing position $k > |\mathcal{O}|$, $\bar{g}_{\sigma(k)} = 0 \implies S_k = S_{|\mathcal{O}|} = 1 \implies w_{\sigma(k)} = 1^p - 1^p = 0$, whereas for $1 \le k \le |\mathcal{O}|$, $w_{\sigma(k)}^{(M)} = S_k^p - S_{k-1}^p = w_{\sigma(k)}^{(|\mathcal{A}|)} = w_{\sigma(k)}^{(|\mathcal{O}|)}$.
3. **Invariance of Observed-Pair Conflict for All $\gamma \ge 0$**:
   In Eq. (4), because $a_u = 0$ for all $u \notin \mathcal{O}$, all terms involving $u \notin \mathcal{O}$ vanish from both numerator and denominator:
   $$\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}^{(M)} = \frac{\sum_{m \in \mathcal{O}} \sum_{n \in \mathcal{O} \setminus \{m\}} (\mu_m \nu_n + \nu_m \mu_n)}{\max(1, |\mathcal{O}|(|\mathcal{O}| - 1))} = \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}^{(|\mathcal{A}|)} = \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}^{(|\mathcal{O}|)} \implies d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}^{(M)}) \equiv d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}^{(|\mathcal{A}|)}).$$
4. **Multiplicative Identity in Geometric-Hesitancy Aggregation**:
   For every $u \notin \mathcal{O}$, $w_u = 0$, $\hat{\mu}_u = 0$, and $\hat{\pi}_u = 1$, so $(1 - \hat{\mu}_u)^{w_u} = 1^0 = 1$ and $(\hat{\pi}_u)^{w_u} = 1^0 = 1$. Thus Eq. (6) reduces identically to the product over $m \in \mathcal{O}$. $\blacksquare$

#### Corollary 2.1 (End-to-End Missing-Modality & Padding-Value Invariance of Concept-Masked `UnifiedDeepFuzzyModel`)
*Consider the full end-to-end `UnifiedDeepFuzzyModel` in inference mode (`eval()`), where each unimodal branch $m \in \{1, \dots, M\}$ applies input sanitization $\tilde{x}_{i,m} = o_{i,m} \cdot \operatorname{nan\_to\_num}(x_{i,m}, \mathrm{nan}=0, \mathrm{posinf}=0, \mathrm{neginf}=0)$ and purely sample-wise unimodal layers (per-sample linear/GELU projections, sample-wise `LayerNorm` if enabled, and unimodal `FuzzyRoughAttentionGate` without cross-sample `BatchNorm` or cross-modal token coupling). `DeepConceptTSKHead` applies the binary concept observation mask $\boldsymbol{m}_{z,i} \in \{0, 1\}^C$ induced by $\boldsymbol{o}_i$ and optional elementwise `clinical_concept_masks` ($m_{z,i,c} = o_{i,m(c)} \cdot m^{\mathrm{clin}}_{i,c}$ for observed clinical measurements vs.\ $m_{z,i,c} = 0$ for any missing modality, masked clinical sub-indicator, or zero-padded slot) to both its Gaussian antecedent distances and linear consequents:*
$$\ell_r(\boldsymbol{z}_i, \boldsymbol{m}_{z,i}) = -\frac{1}{2 \max\!\left(1,\; \sum_{c=1}^C m_{z,i,c}\right)} \sum_{c=1}^C m_{z,i,c}\, g_{r,c} \left(\frac{\tilde{z}_{i,c} - c_{r,c}}{\sigma_{r,c}}\right)^2, \qquad y_r(\boldsymbol{z}_i, \boldsymbol{m}_{z,i}) = w_{r,0} + \sum_{c=1}^C m_{z,i,c}\,(g_{r,c} w_{r,c})\,\tilde{z}_{i,c}, \tag{6b}$$
*with $\tilde{z}_{i,c} = m_{z,i,c} \cdot \operatorname{nan\_to\_num}(z_{i,c}, 0, 0, 0)$, normalized rule firing $\bar{f}_r(\boldsymbol{z}_i, \boldsymbol{m}_{z,i}) = \frac{\exp(\ell_r(\boldsymbol{z}_i, \boldsymbol{m}_{z,i}))}{\sum_{j=1}^R \exp(\ell_j(\boldsymbol{z}_i, \boldsymbol{m}_{z,i}))}$, TSK logit $y_{\mathrm{TSK}}(\boldsymbol{z}_i, \boldsymbol{m}_{z,i}) = \sum_{r=1}^R \bar{f}_r(\boldsymbol{z}_i, \boldsymbol{m}_{z,i}) y_r(\boldsymbol{z}_i, \boldsymbol{m}_{z,i})$, and blended diagnostic probability $p_i = \operatorname{clamp}\!\big((1 - \alpha_{\mathrm{TSK},i}) p_{i,\mathrm{IFS}} + \alpha_{\mathrm{TSK},i} \sigma(y_{\mathrm{TSK}}(\boldsymbol{z}_i, \boldsymbol{m}_{z,i})),\, 0,\, 1\big)$, where $\alpha_{\mathrm{TSK},i} = \sigma(\theta_{\mathrm{TSK}})(1 - \pi_{i,\mathrm{fused}})$. Then for every patient $i$ with unacquired modality subset $\mathcal{U}_i = \{u : o_{i,u} = 0\}$ and acquired subset $\mathcal{A}_i = \mathcal{M} \setminus \mathcal{U}_i$:*
1. **(Identical `0.00` Padding-Value Invariance)**: *For any two arbitrary fill vectors $\{x_{i,u}\}_{u \in \mathcal{U}_i}$ and $\{x_{i,u}'\}_{u \in \mathcal{U}_i}$ in $(\mathbb{R} \cup \{\mathrm{NaN}, \pm\infty\})^{D_u}$ (e.g., zero-imputation, cohort-mean imputation, Gaussian noise, or `NaN`/`±Inf` sentinels) at fixed tensor width, the end-to-end prediction $p_i$ satisfies **exact zero discrepancy** ($|p_i(\{x_{i,m}\}_{m \in \mathcal{A}_i}, \{x_{i,u}\}_{u \in \mathcal{U}_i}) - p_i(\{x_{i,m}\}_{m \in \mathcal{A}_i}, \{x_{i,u}'\}_{u \in \mathcal{U}_i})| \equiv 0.00$), eliminating zero-imputation antecedent drift $(0 - c_{r,c})^2/\sigma_{r,c}^2$ on unobserved concepts. Optional `clinical_concept_masks` likewise gives exact `0.00` invariance to arbitrary fills or perturbations in masked entries of the `clinical_concepts` tensor passed to `DeepConceptTSKHead`, holding all upstream modality encoder inputs $\{x_{i,m}\}$ fixed, or when shared raw clinical variables are simultaneously masked in $x_{i,\mathrm{Clin}}$.*
2. **(Algebraic Sub-Model Restriction Equivalence & Export Precision)**: *Moreover, in exact real arithmetic $p_i$ evaluated on the masked $M$-modality `UnifiedDeepFuzzyModel` is **algebraically identical** to the restricted $(|\mathcal{A}_i|, |\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{obs},i}|)$-submodel retaining only the acquired modality branches $\mathcal{A}_i$ and observed concept coordinates $\mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{obs},i} = \{c \in \{1, \dots, C\} : m_{z,i,c} = 1\}$ (reducing identically to $p_i = 0.5$ when $\mathcal{A}_i = \emptyset$). In finite-precision `float32` arithmetic across variable tensor widths, reduction-order rounding scales proportionally with parameter magnitude ($\le 10^{-6}$ in float32 and $< 10^{-14}$ in float64 at tested default parameter scales; fixed-width padding variations remain exact `0.00` because summation structure is identical). The full-precision masked submodel restriction is algebraically exact in real arithmetic; decimal rounding can change the represented real function. On ADNI Fold 0 validation examples, `.export_clinical_rules(precision=8)` has empirical reconstruction discrepancy $2.384 \times 10^{-7}$ ($\le 2.39 \times 10^{-7}$), scaling empirically as $O(\max_r \|\boldsymbol{w}_r\|_1 \cdot 10^{-8})$ on standardized bounded concepts with non-degenerate widths $\sigma_{r,c}\ge0.20$, not as a uniform bound over unbounded $\boldsymbol{z}\in\mathbb{R}^C$.*

#### Proof of Corollary 2.1
1. 2. By Theorem 2, `IFSChoquetFusion` produces $\tilde{\alpha}_{i,\mathrm{fused}}$ and $p_{i,\mathrm{IFS}}$ identically invariant to both $\{x_{i,u}\}_{u \in \mathcal{U}_i}$ and the missing branches $\mathcal{U}_i$. If $\mathcal{A}_i = \emptyset$, then $\tilde{\alpha}_{i,\mathrm{fused}} = \langle 0, 0, 1\rangle \implies \pi_{i,\mathrm{fused}} = 1 \implies \alpha_{\mathrm{TSK},i} = \sigma(\theta_{\mathrm{TSK}})(1 - 1) = 0$ and $p_i = p_{i,\mathrm{IFS}} = 0.5$.

When $\mathcal{A}_i \neq \emptyset$, for every unobserved concept $c \notin \mathcal{C}_{\mathrm{obs},i}$ ($m_{z,i,c} = 0$), $\tilde{z}_{i,c} = 0$ independently of any head-input padding value, and multiplying both the Gaussian squared-deviation summand and the consequent summand in Eq. (6b) by $m_{z,i,c} = 0$ annihilates $c$'s contribution identically ($0 \cdot g_{r,c}(\tilde{z}_{i,c} - c_{r,c})^2/\sigma_{r,c}^2 = 0$ and $0 \cdot g_{r,c} w_{r,c} \tilde{z}_{i,c} = 0$). Holding the upstream encoder inputs $\{x_{i,m}\}$ fixed (or simultaneously masking shared raw clinical variables in $x_{i,\mathrm{Clin}}$), this also removes arbitrary perturbations in masked entries of the `clinical_concepts` tensor; masking that tensor alone does not mask the encoder input. ### Theorem 3 (Partial-Order Monotonicity & Idempotency)
*Let $\preceq_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ be the Atanassov partial order on $\Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ along constant-certainty (iso-hesitancy) contours $r_m = 1 - \pi_m = \mu_m + \nu_m \in (0, 1)$:*
$$\tilde{\alpha}_m = \langle \mu_m, \nu_m, \pi_m \rangle \preceq_{\mathrm{IFN}} \tilde{\alpha}_m' = \langle \mu_m', \nu_m', \pi_m \rangle \iff \mu_m \le \mu_m' \;\text{ and }\; \nu_m = r_m - \mu_m \ge \nu_m' = r_m - \mu_m'.$$
*Suppose all active modalities $k \in \mathcal{O}$ satisfy $\hat{\pi}_k = 1 - d r_k > 0$ (which holds automatically whenever $r_k \in (0, 1)$, as guaranteed by Definition 1 in real arithmetic, or whenever $\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0$). Let $F_\mu(\boldsymbol{\mu}; \boldsymbol{r}, d) = 1 - \prod_{j \in \mathcal{O}}(1 - d\mu_j)^{w_j(\boldsymbol{r})}$ and $F_\nu(\boldsymbol{\nu}; \boldsymbol{r}, d) = \prod_{j \in \mathcal{O}}(1 - d(r_j - \nu_j))^{w_j(\boldsymbol{r})} - \prod_{j \in \mathcal{O}}(1 - d r_j)^{w_j(\boldsymbol{r})}$ denote the aggregation maps under fixed certainty vector $\boldsymbol{r}$ and frozen conflict discount $d \in (0, 1]$. Then:*
1. **(Strict Monotonicity at Fixed Conflict Discount)**: *For every active modality $m \in \mathcal{O}$ ($w_m > 0$):*
   $$\left.\frac{\partial F_\mu}{\partial \mu_m}\right|_{\boldsymbol{r}, d} = \left.\frac{\partial F_\nu}{\partial \nu_m}\right|_{\boldsymbol{r}, d} = w_m d \frac{1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}}{1 - \hat{\mu}_m} > 0, \qquad \left.\frac{\partial \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}}{\partial \mu_m}\right|_{\boldsymbol{r}, d} = 0. \tag{7a}$$
   *When $\gamma > 0$ and $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}(\boldsymbol{\mu})$ varies jointly with $\mu_m$ along $\nu_m = r_m - \mu_m$ (with $|\mathcal{O}| \ge 2$), the full conflict-coupled derivative is:*
   $$\left.\frac{d \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}}{d \mu_m}\right|_{\boldsymbol{r}, \boldsymbol{\mu}_{-m}} = (1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}})d\left[\frac{w_m}{1 - d\mu_m} - \gamma \frac{\partial \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}{\partial \mu_m}\sum_{j \in \mathcal{O}} \frac{w_j \mu_j}{1 - d\mu_j}\right], \qquad \frac{\partial \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}{\partial \mu_m} = \frac{2}{|\mathcal{O}|(|\mathcal{O}|-1)}\sum_{j \in \mathcal{O}\setminus\{m\}}(\nu_j - \mu_j). \tag{7b}$$
2. **(Idempotency)**: *If all active modalities agree identically ($\tilde{\alpha}_m = \langle \mu_0, \nu_0, \pi_0 \rangle \;\forall m \in \mathcal{O}$) and $\gamma = 0$, then $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}(\tilde{\alpha}_1, \dots, \tilde{\alpha}_M) = \langle \mu_0, \nu_0, \pi_0 \rangle$.*

#### Proof of Theorem 3
1. **Strict Monotonicity**:
   Because both the restricted base measure $\bar{g}_j$ in Eq. (2) and the Choquet sorting key $s_j = r_j a_j$ depend solely on the non-vacuity masses $\{r_j = 1 - \pi_j\}_{j=1}^M$, holding $\boldsymbol{r}$ fixed implies $\frac{\partial w_j}{\partial \mu_m}\big|_{\boldsymbol{r}} = 0$ for all $j, m$. Moreover, at fixed $d \in (0, 1]$, $\hat{\pi}_j = 1 - d r_j$ is constant for all $j \in \mathcal{O}$, so $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = \prod_{j \in \mathcal{O}} \hat{\pi}_j^{w_j} > 0$ satisfies $\frac{\partial \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}}{\partial \mu_m}\big|_{\boldsymbol{r}, d} = 0$, and $1 - F_\mu = \prod_{j \in \mathcal{O}}(1 - \hat{\mu}_j)^{w_j} \ge \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} > 0$.
   Differentiating $\ln(1 - F_\mu) = \sum_{j \in \mathcal{O}} w_j \ln(1 - d \mu_j)$ with respect to $\mu_m$ at fixed $(\boldsymbol{r}, d)$ yields:
   $$-\frac{1}{1 - F_\mu} \left.\frac{\partial F_\mu}{\partial \mu_m}\right|_{\boldsymbol{r}, d} = -\frac{w_m d}{1 - d \mu_m} \implies \left.\frac{\partial F_\mu}{\partial \mu_m}\right|_{\boldsymbol{r}, d} = w_m d \frac{1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}}{1 - \hat{\mu}_m} > 0.$$
   Along the constant-certainty contour $\mu_m = r_m - \nu_m$ ($d\mu_m = -d\nu_m$), since $F_\nu = 1 - F_\mu - \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$ and $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$ is constant at fixed $(\boldsymbol{r}, d)$, the chain rule gives $\left.\frac{\partial F_\nu}{\partial \nu_m}\right|_{\boldsymbol{r}, d} = \left(-\frac{\partial F_\mu}{\partial \mu_m}\right)(-1) = \left.\frac{\partial F_\mu}{\partial \mu_m}\right|_{\boldsymbol{r}, d} > 0$.
   When $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}$ varies with $\mu_m$ along $\nu_m = r_m - \mu_m$, differentiating $\ln(1 - \mu_{\mathrm{fused}}) = \sum_{j \in \mathcal{O}} w_j \ln(1 - e^{-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}\mu_j)$ via the chain rule $\frac{\partial d}{\partial \mu_m} = -\gamma d \frac{\partial \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}{\partial \mu_m}$ and $\frac{\partial}{\partial \mu_m}(\mu_m \nu_j + (r_m - \mu_m)\mu_j) = \nu_j - \mu_j$ yields Eq. (7b).
2. **Idempotency**:
   When $\tilde{\alpha}_m = \langle \mu_0, \nu_0, \pi_0 \rangle$ for all $m \in \mathcal{O}$ and $\gamma = 0$ ($d = 1$), $\sum_{m \in \mathcal{O}} w_m = 1$ gives:
   $$\mu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1 - \prod_{m \in \mathcal{O}} (1 - \mu_0)^{w_m} = 1 - (1 - \mu_0)^{\sum_{m \in \mathcal{O}} w_m} = \mu_0, \qquad \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = \prod_{m \in \mathcal{O}} \pi_0^{w_m} = \pi_0, \qquad \nu_{\mathrm{fused}} = 1 - \mu_0 - \pi_0 = \nu_0. \quad \blacksquare$$

#### Remark 3 (Coupled Conflict Sign Boundary & Positive Conflict on Mixed Identical Inputs)
1. **Coupled Conflict-Monotonicity Boundary in Eq. (7b)**: When conflict penalty $\gamma > 0$ varies $d(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}})$ jointly with $\mu_m$, the total derivative $\left.\frac{d\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}}{d\mu_m}\right|_{\boldsymbol{r},\boldsymbol{\mu}_{-m}}$ in Eq. (7b) stays strictly positive whenever $\gamma \frac{\partial\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}{\partial\mu_m}\sum_{j\in\mathcal{O}}\frac{w_j\mu_j}{1-d\mu_j} < \frac{w_m}{1-d\mu_m}$ (holding unconditionally for every $\gamma \ge 0$ when $\sum_{j\in\mathcal{O}\setminus\{m\}}(\nu_j - \mu_j) \le 0$). Only when other modalities strongly refute ($\sum_{j\neq m}(\nu_j - \mu_j) > 0$) and $\gamma$ exceeds that threshold can rising $\mu_m$ trigger conflict-discount attenuation sufficient to decrease $\mu_{\mathrm{fused}}$ while inflating hesitancy $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$.
2. **Idempotency Under Mixed Identical Evidence ($\mu_0 > 0, \nu_0 > 0$)**: For identical active triplets $\langle \mu_0, \nu_0, \pi_0\rangle$ with $|\mathcal{O}| \ge 2$, observed-pair cross-polarity contradiction evaluates to $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = 2\mu_0\nu_0 > 0$ whenever both $\mu_0 > 0$ and $\nu_0 > 0$. For $|\mathcal{O}|=1$, $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}=0$ and exact pass-through holds for every $\gamma\ge0$. For identical multi-modality inputs, exact idempotency therefore requires $\gamma = 0$ (or unipolar consensus $\mu_0\nu_0 = 0$), whereas $\gamma > 0$ intentionally discounts $d = \exp(-2\gamma\mu_0\nu_0) < 1$ to preserve intra-modality ambiguity inside $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$.

### Theorem 4 (Conflict-Driven Epistemic Hesitancy Inflation & Safety Lower Bound)
*For any conflict penalty $\gamma > 0$ and any set of active modalities $\mathcal{O}$ ($|\mathcal{O}| \ge 2$), holding the modality non-vacuity masses $r_m = 1 - \pi_m = \mu_m + \nu_m \in (0, 1)$ (and hence the induced Choquet weights $w_m$) fixed while varying the directional allocation $(\mu_m, \nu_m)$ and observed-pair conflict $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}$:*
1. **(Strict Hesitancy Inflation Along Iso-Non-Vacuity Contours)**: *Because Choquet weights $w_m$ depend solely on $\{r_j, g_j^0\}$, $\left.\frac{\partial w_m}{\partial \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}\right|_{\{r_j\}} \equiv 0$, and the fused epistemic hesitancy $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}}$ is a strictly increasing function of observed-pair conflict $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}$:*
   $$\left.\frac{\partial \ln \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}}{\partial \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}\right|_{\{r_j\}} = \gamma \sum_{m \in \mathcal{O}} w_m \frac{r_m e^{-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}}{1 - r_m e^{-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}} > 0, \qquad \left.\frac{\partial \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}}{\partial \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}\right|_{\{r_j\}} = \pi_{\mathrm{fused}} \left.\frac{\partial \ln \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}}{\partial \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}\right|_{\{r_j\}} > 0. \tag{8}$$
2. **(Explicit Conflict Lower Bound)**: *Let $r_{\max} = \max_{m \in \mathcal{O}} r_m = 1 - \pi_{\min} < 1$. Then for all $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} \in [0, 1]$:*
   $$\pi_{\mathrm{fused}}(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}) \ge 1 - r_{\max} \exp(-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}) > 1 - \exp(-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}) \ge 0 \quad (\text{strictly } > 0 \text{ whenever } \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} > 0). \tag{9}$$
3. **(Resolution of Zadeh's High-Conflict Uncertainty Collapse)**: *Under total contradiction between two high-confidence binary modalities $\tilde{\alpha}_1 = \langle 1-\epsilon, 0, \epsilon \rangle$ and $\tilde{\alpha}_2 = \langle 0, 1-\epsilon, \epsilon \rangle$ as $\epsilon \to 0^+$, classical Dempster's rule of combination yields conflict mass $K = (1-\epsilon)^2$ and normalized uncertainty $u_{\mathrm{DS}}(\epsilon) = \frac{\epsilon^2}{1 - (1-\epsilon)^2} = \frac{\epsilon}{2-\epsilon} \to 0$, whereas $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}$ inflates hesitancy to $\lim_{\epsilon \to 0^+} \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}(\epsilon) = 1 - \exp(-\gamma) > 0$.*

#### Proof of Theorem 4
1. By Eqs. (2)–(3), $w_m$ is a function of $\{g_j^0 r_j a_j\}_{j=1}^M$ alone; holding $\{r_j\}$ fixed implies $\left.\frac{\partial w_m}{\partial \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}\right|_{\{r_j\}} = 0$. Taking the logarithm of $\pi_{\mathrm{fused}} = \prod_{m \in \mathcal{O}} (1 - r_m e^{-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}})^{w_m}$ gives:
   $$\ln \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}(\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}) = \sum_{m \in \mathcal{O}} w_m \ln\left(1 - r_m e^{-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}}\right).$$
   Differentiating with respect to $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}$ at fixed $\{r_j\}$ yields Eq. (8) directly, where every term in the sum is strictly positive because $w_m > 0$, $r_m \in (0, 1)$, and $\gamma > 0$.
2. Since $r_m \le r_{\max} < 1$ for all $m \in \mathcal{O}$, we have $1 - r_m e^{-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}} \ge 1 - r_{\max} e^{-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}} > 1 - e^{-\gamma \mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}}} \ge 0$. Raising to $w_m \ge 0$ with $\sum_{m \in \mathcal{O}} w_m = 1$ yields Eq. (9).
3. For $\tilde{\alpha}_1 = \langle 1-\epsilon, 0, \epsilon \rangle$ and $\tilde{\alpha}_2 = \langle 0, 1-\epsilon, \epsilon \rangle$, Dempster's rule assigns conflicting mass $K = (1-\epsilon)^2$ and frame-of-discernment mass $m_{1\oplus 2}(\Theta) = \frac{\epsilon^2}{1 - K} = \frac{\epsilon^2}{2\epsilon - \epsilon^2} = \frac{\epsilon}{2 - \epsilon} \to 0$ as $\epsilon \to 0^+$. Under $\mathcal{F}_{\mathrm{IFC}}$, $\mathcal{C}_{\mathcal{O}} = (1-\epsilon)^2 \to 1$ and $r_1 = r_2 = 1-\epsilon \to 1$, so $\lim_{\epsilon \to 0^+} \pi_{\mathrm{fused}}(\epsilon) = 1 - e^{-\gamma} > 0$. $\blacksquare$

### Theorem 5 (Soft-{\L}ukasiewicz Fuzzy-Rough Inclusion & Granular Monotonicity of T-Norm Dependency)

#### Part I (Projected Soft-{\L}ukasiewicz Inclusion Envelope in `DifferentiableFuzzyRoughLayer` & `FuzzyRoughAttentionGate`)
*Let $X = [x_1, \dots, x_N]^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times D}$ be sanitized feature tokens ($\operatorname{nan\_to\_num}(x_i, 0, 0, 0)$) with projected representations $\tilde{x}_i = W_{\mathrm{proj}} x_i + b_{\mathrm{proj}} \in \mathbb{R}^{D_p}$, active token mask $t_i \in \{0, 1\}$, valid neighborhood $V_i = \{j \in \{1, \dots, N\} : \max(t_j, \delta_{ij}) = 1\}$ ($i \in V_i$), and reflexive scaled-$L_1$ fuzzy similarity relation $R_{ij} = \max(t_i t_j, \delta_{ij})\exp\!\left(-\frac{1}{D_p \sigma}\|\tilde{x}_i - \tilde{x}_j\|_1\right) \in [0, 1]$ satisfying $R_{ii} = 1$, where $\sigma = \operatorname{Softplus}(\theta_\sigma) + 0.1 > 0$. Let $A = (A_1, \dots, A_N)^\top \in [0, 1]^N$ be a target concept membership vector. Define the {\L}ukasiewicz implication $I_{ij} = \min(1, 1 - R_{ij} + A_j)$ and {\L}ukasiewicz t-norm $T_{ij} = \max(0, R_{ij} + A_j - 1)$, and let the piecewise-differentiable (subdifferentiable a.e.) projected Soft-{\L}ukasiewicz approximations over $j \in V_i$ with smoothing coefficient $\beta \in [0, 1]$ and temperature $\tau > 0$ ($\tau_{\mathrm{eff}} = \max(\tau, 10^{-30})$) be:*
$$\underline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i = \min\left(A_i,\; (1 - \beta)\min_{j \in V_i} I_{ij} + \beta \sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^- I_{ij}\right), \qquad \overline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i = \max\left(A_i,\; (1 - \beta)\max_{j \in V_i} T_{ij} + \beta \sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^+ T_{ij}\right), \tag{10}$$
*where $\omega_{ij}^- = \frac{\exp(-(I_{ij} - \min_{k \in V_i} I_{ik})/\tau)}{\sum_{k \in V_i} \exp(-(I_{ik} - \min_{l \in V_i} I_{il})/\tau)}$ and $\omega_{ij}^+ = \frac{\exp((T_{ij} - \max_{k \in V_i} T_{ik})/\tau)}{\sum_{k \in V_i} \exp((T_{ik} - \max_{l \in V_i} T_{il})/\tau)}$ for $j \in V_i$ (and $\omega_{ij}^\pm = 0$ for $j \notin V_i$). Then for all $\beta \in [0, 1]$ and $\tau > 0$:*
$$0 \le \underline{R}_{\mathrm{exact}}(A)_i \le \underline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i \le A_i \le \overline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i \le \overline{R}_{\mathrm{exact}}(A)_i \le 1, \tag{11}$$
*and the Fuzzy Boundary Region $\operatorname{BND}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i = \overline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i - \underline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i \ge 0$ is everywhere non-negative.*

#### Part II (Granular Monotonicity of Fuzzy-Rough Dependency under Pointwise T-Norm Refinement in `FuzzyRoughFeatureReducer`)
*Let $\mathcal{F} = \{1, \dots, D\}$ be a candidate feature universe on $N$ training instances $\{x_i\}_{i=1}^N \subset \mathbb{R}^D$ with decision co-membership $y_{ij} = 1 - |y_i - y_j| \in [0, 1]$, where each single attribute $k \in \mathcal{F}$ defines an unsupervised reflexive fuzzy similarity $R_{\{k\}}(x_i, x_j) \in [0, 1]$ ($R_{\{k\}}(x_i, x_i) = 1$, e.g., $R_{\{k\}}(x_i, x_j) = \exp(-|x_{i,k} - x_{j,k}|/\sigma_k)$ or $\max(0, 1 - |x_{i,k} - x_{j,k}|/\sigma_k)$ with unsupervised per-feature dispersion $\sigma_k > 0$). For any non-empty attribute subset $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{F}$, construct the joint fuzzy indiscernibility relation via a commutative, associative, monotone triangular norm (T-norm) $\top : [0, 1]^2 \to [0, 1]$ with neutral element $1$ ($\top(a, 1) = a$):*
$$R_{\mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_j) = \underset{k \in \mathcal{B}}{\bigtop}\, R_{\{k\}}(x_i, x_j) \in [0, 1], \tag{12}$$
*such as the **Gödel minimum T-norm** $R_{\mathcal{B}}^{\min}(x_i, x_j) = \min_{k \in \mathcal{B}} R_{\{k\}}(x_i, x_j)$, the **algebraic product T-norm** $R_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{prod}}(x_i, x_j) = \prod_{k \in \mathcal{B}} R_{\{k\}}(x_i, x_j)$ (equal to $\exp\!\big(-\sum_{k \in \mathcal{B}} |x_{i,k} - x_{j,k}|/\sigma_k\big)$ specifically for the exponential similarity kernel, not in general for triangular similarity), or the **{\L}ukasiewicz T-norm** $R_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{Luk}}(x_i, x_j) = \max\!\big(0, \sum_{k \in \mathcal{B}} R_{\{k\}}(x_i, x_j) - |\mathcal{B}| + 1\big)$. Then:*
1. **(Pointwise T-Norm Granular Refinement Premise)**: *For all nested subsets $\emptyset \neq \mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{B}' \subseteq \mathcal{F}$ and every sample pair $(x_i, x_j)$, adding attributes refines indiscernibility **pointwise**:*
   $$0 \le R_{\mathcal{B}'}(x_i, x_j) \le R_{\mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_j) \le 1, \qquad R_{\mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_i) = R_{\mathcal{B}'}(x_i, x_i) = 1. \tag{13}$$
   *(Note: Subset-averaged or label-rescaled $L_1$ distances $\frac{1}{|\mathcal{B}|}\sum_{k \in \mathcal{B}} |x_{i,k} - x_{j,k}|$ do NOT satisfy Eq. (13) when an added attribute has smaller pairwise separation than the current average, which can otherwise violate reduct monotonicity.)*
2. **(Granular Monotonicity of Positive Dependency & Boundary Contraction)**: *Let $I_{\mathcal{B}}(i, j) = \min(1, 1 - R_{\mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_j) + y_{ij})$ and $T_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{opp}}(i, j) = \max(0, R_{\mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_j) - y_{ij})$ over comparison neighborhood $V_i \subseteq \{1, \dots, N\}$ ($V_i = \{1, \dots, N\} \setminus \{i\}$ or $\{1, \dots, N\}$, $|V_i| \ge 1$). Under both the exact Dubois–Prade operator ($\underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D)_i = \min_{j \in V_i} I_{\mathcal{B}}(i, j)$, $\overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i = \max_{j \in V_i} T_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{opp}}(i, j)$) and its monotone smooth LogSumExp relaxation ($\underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{LSE},\tau}(D)_i = \min\!\big(1, -\tau \ln \frac{1}{|V_i|}\sum_{j \in V_i} e^{-I_{\mathcal{B}}(i,j)/\tau}\big)$, $\overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{LSE},\tau}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i = \max\!\big(0, \tau \ln \frac{1}{|V_i|}\sum_{j \in V_i} e^{T_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{opp}}(i,j)/\tau}\big)$ for any $\tau > 0$, or any convex blend $(1-\beta)\underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}} + \beta \underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{LSE},\tau}$ with $\beta \in [0, 1]$), whenever $\mathcal{B} \subseteq \mathcal{B}'$ satisfies the pointwise refinement premise $R_{\mathcal{B}'}(x_i, x_j) \le R_{\mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_j)$:*
   $$\underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D)_i \le \underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}'}(D)_i \implies \gamma_{\mathcal{B}}(D) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D)_i \;\le\; \gamma_{\mathcal{B}'}(D) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}'}(D)_i, \tag{14a}$$
   $$\overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}'}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i \le \overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i \implies \operatorname{BND}_{\mathcal{B}'}(D) \;\le\; \operatorname{BND}_{\mathcal{B}}(D), \tag{14b}$$
   *where $\operatorname{BND}_{\mathcal{B}}(D) = \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i\big(1 - \frac{1}{2}\underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D)_i\big)$ (or $\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i$, or complementary $1 - \gamma_{\mathcal{B}}(D)$). In the executed `FuzzyRoughFeatureReducer` implementation (`deepfuzzy/rough_reducer.py`), training batches exceeding `max_samples` are class-stratified subsampled **without replacement** (`_stratified_subsample_indices`), each feature $k$ is standardized unsupervised via $\tilde{x}_{i,k} = (x_{i,k} - \operatorname{med}_k)/s_k$ (using Gaussian-consistent $\mathrm{MAD}_k = 1.4826\,\mathrm{med}_i|x_{i,k}-\mathrm{med}_k|$: $s_k = 4\cdot\operatorname{MAD}_k$ when $\operatorname{MAD}_k > 10^{-6}$, falling back to $s_k = 4\cdot\operatorname{SD}_k$ when $\operatorname{MAD}_k \le 10^{-6} < \operatorname{SD}_k$, and to $s_k = 4.0$ when both $\operatorname{MAD}_k \le 10^{-6}$ and $\operatorname{SD}_k \le 10^{-6}$ [including constant or all-missing coordinates]) with Laplace similarity $R_{\{k\}}(x_i,x_j) = \exp(-|\tilde{x}_{i,k} - \tilde{x}_{j,k}|/\sigma)$ ($\sigma = 0.85$), non-self comparison neighborhood $V_i = \{1,\dots,N\}\setminus\{i\}$ ($|V_i| = N - 1$), soft-infimum temperature $\tau_{\mathrm{reducer}} = 0.08$, satisfying $0 \le \min_{j\in V_i} I_{\mathcal{B}}(i,j) \le \underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{LSE},\tau}(D)_i \le \frac{1}{|V_i|}\sum_{j\in V_i} I_{\mathcal{B}}(i,j) \le 1$, and forward-greedy ranking by normalized excess dependency $\widehat{\Delta}\gamma_{\mathcal{B}}(D) = \frac{\gamma_{\mathcal{B}}(D) - \gamma_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{null}}(D)}{\max(10^{-6}, 1 - \gamma_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{null}}(D))} + 10^{-4}\gamma_{\mathcal{B}}(D)$ across stratified without-replacement subsample views ($5$ on ADNI MRI, $8$ on TCGA RNA-Seq; $85\%$ per view below `max_samples`). #### Proof of Theorem 5
1. **Proof of Part I (Soft-{\L}ukasiewicz Inclusion Envelope)**:
   - Since $i \in V_i$ and $R_{ii} = 1$, we have $I_{ii} = \min(1, 1 - 1 + A_i) = A_i$ and $T_{ii} = \max(0, 1 + A_i - 1) = A_i$. Hence the exact Dubois–Prade approximations over $V_i$ satisfy $\underline{R}_{\mathrm{exact}}(A)_i = \min_{j \in V_i} I_{ij} \le I_{ii} = A_i$ and $\overline{R}_{\mathrm{exact}}(A)_i = \max_{j \in V_i} T_{ij} \ge T_{ii} = A_i$.
   - Because $\sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^- = 1$ with $\omega_{ij}^- \ge 0$ (and shift-stabilized working-precision evaluation in `float64`—or `float32` on MPS—via $\min_{k \in V_i} I_{ik} + \sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^-(I_{ij} - \min_{k \in V_i} I_{ik})$ guarantees $\exp(0) = 1$ on extremal elements without denominator underflow for $\tau_{\mathrm{eff}} < 10^{-4}$, half precision, or `torch.autocast`), $\sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^- I_{ij} \ge \min_{j \in V_i} I_{ij}$. For any $\beta \in [0, 1]$, $B_i^- = (1-\beta)\min_{j \in V_i} I_{ij} + \beta \sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^- I_{ij} \ge \min_{j \in V_i} I_{ij}$, so $\underline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i = \min(A_i, B_i^-)$ satisfies $\underline{R}_{\mathrm{exact}}(A)_i \le \underline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i \le A_i$.
   - Dually, $\sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^+ T_{ij} \le \max_{j \in V_i} T_{ij} \implies B_i^+ = (1-\beta)\max_{j \in V_i} T_{ij} + \beta \sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^+ T_{ij} \le \max_{j \in V_i} T_{ij}$, so $\overline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i = \max(A_i, B_i^+)$ satisfies $A_i \le \overline{R}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i \le \overline{R}_{\mathrm{exact}}(A)_i \le 1$, and $\operatorname{BND}_{\tau,\beta}(A)_i \ge 0$.
   - *Unimodal `FuzzyRoughAttentionGate` Specialization*: Projecting each modality token $t_m \in \mathbb{R}^{d_e}$ via $w = \operatorname{Softplus}(\theta_w)$ into $G=5$ internal granules $a_{b,m,g} = \sigma((w \odot t_m)W_g + b_g) \in (0, 1)$ anchored at $A_{b,m} = o_{b,m} a_{b,m,0}$ with $R_{b,m,g} = \exp(-\gamma_{\mathrm{FR}}|a_{b,m,g} - A_{b,m}|)$ gives $R_{b,m,0} = 1$ when $o_{b,m} = 1$ (preserving Eq. (11)) and zeros all outputs identically via the outer mask $o_{b,m}$ when $o_{b,m} = 0$, with zero cross-modality leakage.
2. **Proof of Part II (Pointwise T-Norm Refinement & Granular Monotonicity)**:
   - For any T-norm $\top$ and any $a, b \in [0, 1]$, monotonicity of $\top$ in its second argument together with neutrality of $1$ implies $\top(a, b) \le \top(a, 1) = a$ (specifically: $\min(a, b) \le a$ for Gödel; $ab \le a$ for product; $\max(0, a + b - 1) \le \max(0, a) = a$ for {\L}ukasiewicz). By induction over $\mathcal{B}' \setminus \mathcal{B} = \{k_1, \dots, k_m\}$, $R_{\mathcal{B}'}(x_i, x_j) = \top\big(R_{\mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_j), R_{\mathcal{B}' \setminus \mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_j)\big) \le R_{\mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_j)$ holds pointwise for every pair $(x_i, x_j)$, while $\top(1, 1) = 1$ preserves reflexivity $R_{\mathcal{B}'}(x_i, x_i) = 1$.
   - Fix any pair $(i, j)$. Since $r \mapsto \min(1, 1 - r + y_{ij})$ is monotonically non-increasing in $r$ and $r \mapsto \max(0, r - y_{ij})$ is monotonically non-decreasing in $r$, the pointwise inequality $R_{\mathcal{B}'}(x_i, x_j) \le R_{\mathcal{B}}(x_i, x_j)$ immediately yields:
     $$I_{\mathcal{B}}(i, j) \le I_{\mathcal{B}'}(i, j) \qquad \text{and} \qquad T_{\mathcal{B}'}^{\mathrm{opp}}(i, j) \le T_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{opp}}(i, j) \qquad \forall j \in V_i.$$
   - Taking coordinatewise nondecreasing extrema over $j \in V_i$ preserves both inequalities: for exact hard $\min/\max$, $\min_{j \in V_i} I_{\mathcal{B}}(i, j) \le \min_{j \in V_i} I_{\mathcal{B}'}(i, j)$ and $\max_{j \in V_i} T_{\mathcal{B}'}^{\mathrm{opp}}(i, j) \le \max_{j \in V_i} T_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{opp}}(i, j)$; and for the unclamped LogSumExp operators in Part II, every partial derivative is strictly positive everywhere in real arithmetic:
     $$\frac{\partial}{\partial I_{ij}}\left(-\tau \ln \frac{1}{|V_i|}\sum_{k \in V_i} e^{-I_{ik}/\tau}\right) = \frac{e^{-I_{ij}/\tau}}{\sum_{k \in V_i} e^{-I_{ik}/\tau}} > 0, \qquad \frac{\partial}{\partial T_{ij}^{\mathrm{opp}}}\left(\tau \ln \frac{1}{|V_i|}\sum_{k \in V_i} e^{T_{ik}^{\mathrm{opp}}/\tau}\right) = \frac{e^{T_{ij}^{\mathrm{opp}}/\tau}}{\sum_{k \in V_i} e^{T_{ik}^{\mathrm{opp}}/\tau}} > 0,$$
     The outer $[0,1]$ clamp preserves coordinatewise nondecreasing order and strict increase wherever its output lies in $(0,1)$. Zero subgradients can occur where clipped at $\{0,1\}$ or through finite-precision weight underflow. Consequently, $\underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{LSE},\tau}(D)_i \le \underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}'}^{\mathrm{LSE},\tau}(D)_i$ and $\overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}'}^{\mathrm{LSE},\tau}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i \le \overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}^{\mathrm{LSE},\tau}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i$ hold identically for all $\tau > 0$ (distinct from Part I's Boltzmann expectation $\sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^- I_{ij}$, which can violate coordinatewise monotonicity at small temperatures $\tau < 1$ (whereas on $[0,1]$ it is coordinatewise nondecreasing for $\tau \ge 1$, though lacking the unclamped LogSumExp's strictly positive real-arithmetic derivatives) and approximates $\min_{j \in V_i} I_{ij}$ within $0 \le \sum_{j \in V_i} \omega_{ij}^- I_{ij} - \min_{j \in V_i} I_{ij} \le \tau W(e^{-1}(|V_i|-1)) = O(\tau)$ as $\tau \to 0^+$).
   - Finally, because $0 \le \overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}'}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i \le \overline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D_{\mathrm{opp}})_i$ and $0 < 1 - \frac{1}{2}\underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}'}(D)_i \le 1 - \frac{1}{2}\underline{R}_{\mathcal{B}}(D)_i \le 1$, multiplying the two non-negative inequalities yields $\operatorname{BND}_{\mathcal{B}'}(D)_i \le \operatorname{BND}_{\mathcal{B}}(D)_i$ for every $i$, and averaging over $i = 1, \dots, N$ proves Eqs. (14a)–(14b). $\blacksquare$

#### Remark 4 (`PythagoreanFuzzyTTALoss` Quadratic Disk Residual on Linear-Simplex Inputs)
When an intuitionistic triplet $(\mu, \nu, \pi_{\mathrm{lin}}) \in \Delta_{\mathrm{IFN}}$ ($\mu + \nu + \pi_{\mathrm{lin}} = 1$) is mapped onto Yager's Pythagorean disk $\mu_P^2 + \nu_P^2 \le 1$ inside `PythagoreanFuzzyTTALoss` ($\mu_P = \mu s_P, \nu_P = \nu s_P$ with $s_P = 1 - 0.5\operatorname{clamp}(\pi_{\mathrm{lin}}, 0, 0.99)$), the linear constraint $\mu_P + \nu_P \le \mu + \nu = 1 - \pi_{\mathrm{lin}} \le 1$ already satisfies $\mu_P^2 + \nu_P^2 \le 1$ without requiring radial norm division. Its quadratic residual $q_P = 1 - \mu_P^2 - \nu_P^2 = 1 - (\mu_P + \nu_P)^2 + 2\mu_P\nu_P$ simultaneously penalizes both vacuous ignorance ($1 - \mu_P - \nu_P$) and indeterminate mixed polarity ($2\mu_P\nu_P$), whereas radial norm division only activates for standalone external inputs whose post-shrinkage squared norm satisfies $\mu_P^2 + \nu_P^2 > 1$. Any already Pythagorean-valid input shrunk by $s_P \le 1$ remains inside the unit disk. For $q_P(x_i)=\max(0,1-\mu_P(x_i)^2-\nu_P(x_i)^2)$, the detached reliability mask uses $\tilde{\pi}_P(x_i) = \min\!\big(1, \sqrt{q_P(x_i) + 10^{-7}}\big)$, matching `deepfuzzy/operators.py`.
